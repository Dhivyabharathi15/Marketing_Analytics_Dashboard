# Marketing_Analytics_Dashboard

📈 **Marketing Analytics Dashboard: A Power BI Dashboard Guide**

** Overview**

This repository contains the resources and Power BI file for building a comprehensive Marketing Analytics Dashboard.
The dashboard provides actionable insights into campaign performance, audience behavior, spending trends, and video engagement metrics.
It helps marketers and analysts monitor campaign efficiency, identify top-performing ads, and optimize marketing strategies based on data-driven decisions.

**Dashboard Sections**

 **Daily Lead Trends**

_Line Chart_

Visualizes daily, monthly, and yearly lead generation.

Helps identify high-performing days and seasonal lead variations.

**Campaign Flow Funnel**

_Funnel Chart_

Tracks campaign journey from Impressions → Reach → Clicks → Link Clicks → Leads.

Highlights conversion efficiency across funnel stages.

**Average Video by Year & Month**

_Line Chart_

Displays video performance trends across time.

Useful for tracking audience engagement growth.

**Gender Distribution**

_Donut Chart_

Represents audience distribution by gender and age group.

Helps in demographic-based targeting analysis.

**KPI Cards**

_Card Visuals_

Metrics: Leads, Total Leads, Total Spent, Impressions, Reach, Clicks, Link Clicks, Total Video, Avg Play Time, Total ThruPlays, Avg ThruPlay.

Provides quick overview of overall marketing health.

**Slicers**

_Interactive Filters_

Fields: Day, City, Gender, Date, AdSet Name, Campaign Name.

Allows dynamic exploration of campaign data.

**Leads by Age Group**

_Donut Chart_

Shows how different age groups contribute to lead generation.

**Average Conversion Rate Ranking**

_Gauge Chart_

Displays campaign conversion performance ranking.

**Amount Spent**

_Line Chart_

Tracks marketing expenditure over months and years.

Supports spend optimization analysis.

**Impression vs Reach by Day**

_Line Chart_

Compares daily impressions and reach.

Identifies visibility and engagement patterns.

**Age vs Gender – Avg CTR & CPC**

_Matrix Table_

Analyzes average CTR and CPC by age and gender.

Useful for assessing cost-effectiveness and audience engagement.

**Amount Spent Over Time**

_Line Chart_

Displays year-wise spending trends for budget planning.

**Trend of ThruPlays vs Cost per ThruPlay**

_Line Chart_

Shows relationship between ThruPlay counts and their associated costs.

**Total Video Plays & ThruPlays by Campaign**

_Clustered Column Chart_

Compares total video plays and thruplays across campaigns.

**Video Played by Age Group**

_Donut Chart_

Represents audience engagement based on age segmentation.

**Campaign Overview**

_Decomposition Tree_

Breaks down campaign hierarchy by: Leads → Campaign → Ad Set → Ad Name → Impressions → Video Name.

Helps explore granular campaign insights.

**Campaign Performance Table**

_Matrix_

Shows detailed metrics like Reach, Impressions, Clicks, Link Clicks, CPC, CTR, Leads, and Amount.

Provides a summarized campaign performance overview.

**Usage**

_Explore the dashboard to:_

Identify top-performing campaigns and demographics.

Compare engagement vs spend patterns.

Filter by campaign name, city, or gender for deep analysis.


**Key Insights**

Revealed audience segments with the highest engagement.

Tracked spend efficiency and campaign ROI trends.

Improved understanding of video content performance over time.
